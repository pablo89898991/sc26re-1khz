Gyro drift

Put the controller on a table and don’t touch it. Calibrate drift takes about 2 s (on the controller: LB + RB + X + Y). Full calibration also measures the gyro at rest and sets the pitch / yaw / roll dead bands (about 15 s). Reset goes back to the factory drift and turns the dead bands off.

Sticks

Hands off both sticks, then press: their resting point becomes the centre (saved).

Triggers

Soft pull fires when the trigger passes the point you choose, full pull is the click at the end. Trigger mode (per trigger, per profile): Simple threshold — both independent; Hair trigger — soft fires on any further pull and stops on any release (fast semi-auto); Hip fire — a quick pull to the click fires only full (shoot from the hip), a slow pull fires soft (aim) and then full too; Hip fire exclusive — the same but soft and full never together. The hip fire window sets what counts as quick. Analog start / end trims the range Steam sees. Binding the soft pull takes the analog trigger away from Steam.

Gyro mouse — 360° (real-world sensitivity)

In the game look at something you can find again → Start (or LB + RB + View + A) → press the calibration button (set on the gyro page, default A): the camera turns slowly → press it again exactly at 360°. Also used by flick stick and quick turns.

Trackpad pressure

Teaches the controller how hard your pads read when untouched and when pressed firmly — the pressure-based click, “pressed harder than …” activation and Steam’s pad pressure all use it. Follow the steps. Factory clears it; the factory values come back after the controller is turned off and on.

Gyro → right stick: measure the game

For games without mouse support. The controller pushes the right stick by itself while you watch the camera; from that the page works out the game’s dead zone, turn speed, response curve and acceleration and sets gyro as joystick so the camera follows the gyro like a mouse (1:1). Steam Input’s own stick settings are measured too, so keep them as you will play.

Touch sensors

Stick touch (capsense) and grip touch (gripsense) have thresholds instead of a calibration: set them on Controller settings → Touch sensors (higher = less sensitive). The trackpad click point (how far into the calibrated pressure a click is) is there too.

What it is

While this page is connected the controller keeps its gyro on, does not switch itself off and leaves lizard mode (desktop keyboard / mouse emulation) off, so the live highlights and the gyro work without Steam; everything goes back to normal about 10 s after the page is closed.

Profiles stored in the controller turn its inputs into keyboard keys, mouse buttons / movement / scroll and Steam pad buttons. It works without Steam and without installing anything, over the USB cable and the Puck. Needs sc26re firmware v51+ and desktop Chrome, Edge or Brave.

Turning it on

LB + RB + Menu on the controller (high beep = on, low = off) or the Remapper switch at the top. While it is on, every input that has a binding belongs to the remapper: Steam and the game only see what it is mapped to. Inputs without a binding work as usual.

Binding an input

Click it on the picture or in the lists. Press = actions while held (up to 4 at once, e.g. Ctrl + C). Hold adds a second set for holding longer than 0.25 s (a short press then taps the first set). Double adds a set for a quick second press. Start press taps the first set the moment you press. Release press taps a set when you let go — on trackpads only when the finger leaves (or the click ends), so you can slide over the zones and pick one by lifting. Toggle switches the first set on/off with each press. Then press Save to controller.

D-pad, sticks and trackpads

D-pad: 8-way (diagonals press both directions) or 4-way (one direction at a time). Sticks: analog to Steam (with inner / outer dead zones), D-pad 4-way or 8-way (each direction bound like a button, dead zone = how far before it fires), mouse or scroll. Trackpads: to Steam, D-pad 4-way / 8-way, mouse, scroll wheel (circle your finger; each step fires clockwise / counter-clockwise) or gates (4 corners). A trackpad mode works while the pad is touched or only while it is clicked. Click the stick or trackpad to choose; its directions / corners / wheel appear in the list.

Xbox 360 output

Mouse: left / right buttons, middle, back and forward, wheel steps, and “Mouse move” up / down / left / right (moves while held, speed per profile). Middle / back / forward work over the USB cable; the Puck’s mouse only has left and right.

Pad output at the top (applies after turning the controller off and on; USB cable only — the Puck cannot carry it):
Steam Controller — as always. Xbox 360 only — Windows also sees an Xbox 360 controller (its own XInput driver, nothing to install) and the game gets the final pad there: buttons, sticks (incl. trackpad / gyro joystick), analog triggers and the “Pad …” actions; the Steam Controller pad keeps only Steam and QAM so nothing arrives twice. Steam + Xbox 360 — both pads work separately: the Steam Controller pad as usual, the Xbox pad gets only the “Xbox …” actions you bind (Xbox pad only). Keyboard / mouse outputs work in every case. Back buttons and trackpads have no Xbox equivalent: bind them to something.

Mode shifts

Like Steam Input: every group (face buttons, D-pad, each stick, each trackpad, each trigger) can have a mode shift button. While you hold it, the group uses a second set of settings and bindings — e.g. hold LB and the right trackpad turns from mouse into a scroll wheel. Open the group (or one of its buttons), choose the button under “Mode shift”, then switch between Normal and Shifted ⇧ to edit each. The shift button itself is not sent to the game.

More trackpad / stick modes

Joystick (trackpads): the finger position works as a stick for Steam, on the same or the other side, with dead zones, anti dead zone and curve. Flick stick (sticks and trackpads): push past the threshold and the camera turns to face that direction, then rotating keeps turning 1:1 — it uses the gyro mouse “counts per 360°”, so do the 360° calibration first. Directional swipe (trackpads): each swipe fires up / down / left / right — once per touch, or repeatedly along a long swipe if you tick it.

9 zones (grid)

Trackpad mode: the pad is split into 3 × 3 zones by four lines you can move (left, right, top, bottom); each zone — 4 corners, 4 edges and the centre — has its own binding, and zone haptics tick under your finger whenever it crosses into another zone (also for gates and the trackpad D-pad). It works on touch, on click, or only when the pad is pressed harder than the pressure you set.

Profile extras

LED next to the profile name: the controller’s LED takes that colour while the profile is active (and goes back when you switch away or turn the remapper off). Gyro per profile: on the gyro page choose “Settings for: this profile only”, tick “uses its own gyro settings” and set mouse / joystick, activation, ratchet, sensitivity, curves and quick turns for this profile; “turn the gyro on with this profile” switches the gyro output on when the profile becomes active. Ratchet: while its button is held the gyro stops moving, so you can re-centre your hands. Under the picture the Output line shows live what the remapper sends: keys, mouse buttons and movement, wheel, pad / Xbox buttons and active mode shifts.

Unbound inputs do nothing

Per profile: when ticked, Steam and the game only get what the profile maps — every input without a binding (buttons, touch sensors, trackpads left “to Steam”) is dropped. Sticks set to Analog and the Steam button still work.

Outer ring

Sticks and trackpads have an outer ring: its binding fires while the stick / finger is beyond the radius you set (or inside it, with invert) — e.g. sprint when the stick is pushed all the way.

Triggers

Soft pull fires when the trigger passes the point you choose (1–100 %), full pull is the click at the end. Binding the soft pull takes the analog trigger away from Steam.

Haptics

Every binding has its own haptics (off / tick / click / strong) played on that side’s trackpad actuator when it fires; mouse and scroll modes can tick while moving. Gyro mouse haptics are on the gyro page.

Touch sensors

Stick touch (capsense), grip touch (gripsense), trackpad touch and trackpad clicks are bindable like buttons.

Profiles

4 profiles. Choose the active one with Steam + D-pad on the controller (up = 1, right = 2, down = 3, left = 4; the beep gets higher with the number), at the top of “Buttons & profiles”, or with a “Switch to profile” action bound to any input. Export / Import saves a profile to a file.

Gyro

Gyro settings, gyro as mouse (on/off: LB + RB + View) and gyro as a joystick (right or left stick, with dead zone, anti dead zone, full-deflection speed and response curve) are on the “Gyro & gyro mouse” page, the same for every profile. The mouse also has a response curve and acceleration (off by default, so the real-world sensitivity stays 1:1).
