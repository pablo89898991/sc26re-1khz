Steam Controller 2 — 1 kHz tools

Firmware Patcher
Patch the stock controller (IBEX) and Puck (PROTEUS) firmware in your browser: USB 1 kHz, true HA gyro 1000 Hz, Puck poll rate.
Ibex Web Utilities
Flash controller firmware (stock, patched or sc26re) from the browser.
3D Overlay
A 3D Steam Controller that moves with your hands for recordings and streams: every button, sticks, trackpads (finger and pressure), stick touch, grip touch and the gyro. Capture the window in OBS with a chroma key.
Remapper
On-controller remapper for sc26re v51+: profiles stored in the controller map every input (back buttons, touch sensors, sticks, trackpads) to keyboard, mouse and Steam pad buttons — works without Steam.
Report Meter
WebHID meter: report rate, jitter, fresh gyro samples (Chrome/Edge).
Latest sc26re 1 kHz firmware
Built automatically from the master branch. See docs/1KHZ.md.
