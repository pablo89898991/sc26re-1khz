## Fixes

- **Gyro calibration no longer gets lost when you turn the remapper on or off.** A remapper profile can have its own gyro range (for example 2000 °/s while the normal mode uses 250 °/s), and the gyro's zero point is different at each range: on the test controller about 0.07 °/s at 250 °/s and about 1.1 °/s at 2000 °/s. There was one calibration for both, so after switching, the gyro drifted until you calibrated again, and that calibration then was wrong for the other mode. Now each range keeps its own calibration, saved separately and kept after power-off. Calibrate once in each mode you use (normal mode and a profile with its own range).
- **Steam + Y right after lifting the controller off the Puck switches it fully off.** The firmware could take a stray signal on the open charging contacts for the Puck and keep waiting for it. It now counts the Puck as present only while it receives valid data from it.

## Remapper

- On the gyro page, a profile with its own gyro range different from the global one shows a note, so it is clear why the gyro behaves differently in that profile.

## Report meter

- Over the Puck, a new line shows the radio link: channel, reports sent by the controller and received by the page, and radio errors.

## Under the hood

- New diagnostic paths: `sc26re/link` (Puck radio counters) and `sc26re/gbias` (gyro calibration, the sensor's running bias estimate and the active range).
