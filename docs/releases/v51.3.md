## Fixes

- **Haptics stop when they are told to.** A stop (OFF) from the PC did nothing, the same as in Valve's firmware, so apps that play long tones and stop them, like the Steam Controller RC car page, left the motors running for up to half a minute. Over the Puck a haptic report that could not be played also made the controller throw away the rest of the radio packet, so a stop right behind it was lost. Now a stop ends long tones and sweeps at once, nothing else in the packet is lost, and a tone or sweep from the PC lasts at most 3 s unless it is sent again (apps that play continuously resend it anyway). Measured on a controller: the motors are still 0.15 s after the stop.
- **Gyro calibration no longer beeps in a loop.** Steam + X, the report meter and the remapper calibrate while the controller lies still. Some controllers have fast noise of about 1 deg/s on one gyro axis even when they lie still, and the calibration took it for movement and kept starting over. It now judges movement on 50 ms averages, where that noise evens out but a hand or a turn still shows, so it finishes; the drift it measures is the same. Strong shaking still stops it. The automatic calibration uses the same 50 ms averages.
