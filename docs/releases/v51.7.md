## New: custom response curves (4 points, as in Steam)

In the [remapper](https://pablo89898991.github.io/sc26re-1khz/remap.html) you can now draw your own response curve instead of the single "Response curve" exponent:

- **Sticks:** in a stick's options (to Steam, Xbox pad, mouse), **Response → custom curve**. One curve per stick in each profile, used in every mode and layer of that stick.
- **Gyro as mouse:** on the gyro page, **Mouse response → custom curve**. Left to right is how fast you turn the controller (0 to **Curve range**, 360 °/s by default), bottom to top is how fast the cursor moves. The dashed diagonal is 1:1, the same as without a curve. Faster turns follow the last part of the line.
- **Gyro as joystick:** **Joystick response → custom curve**. Left to right is the turn speed from the dead zone to full speed, bottom to top is the stick deflection the game gets.

Drag the 4 points or type their values (0–32767, the same scale as Steam's "Precise adjustment"). There are presets: Linear, Wide, Aggressive, Relaxed and Precise. The gyro curves are global, or the profile's own when the profile has its own gyro settings. Curves are saved with the profile or with Apply / Save on the gyro page, and profile export and import keep them.

Tested on a controller: the stick outputs match the curve exactly at 25, 50, 75 and 100 %, and the stick mouse, gyro mouse and gyro joystick follow it.

## Fixes

- **The gyro could become about 15 times too slow after switching the controller on.** With the gyro's sensor mode set to high-performance, the sensor sometimes started at a different range than the one set, while the firmware converted its readings for the set range. The gyro mouse then moved far too little, and a drift or 360° calibration made in that state was wrong too. The firmware now checks the sensor's range every 100 ms and sets it back if it differs. If your gyro felt slow, do the drift calibration (Steam + X) and the 360° calibration again after updating.
- **Remapper:** saving over the Puck right after another setting could lose part of the data. Curves are now read back after saving and sent again if they differ.

## Under the hood

- New diagnostic path `sc26re/imumode`: the sensor's mode registers, the raw gyro reading and the conversion, and how many times the range had to be set back.
