## Fixes

- **An off controller placed on the Puck now only charges.** Before, it switched fully on. Steam + Y cuts the battery off completely, and the charger wakes the controller the same way whether you press Steam or the Puck gives its first bit of power, so at that moment the firmware cannot tell which it was, and the Puck itself stays silent for about 11 s. Now, after such a start, if the Puck's link appears within the first 25 s and nobody has touched the controller, it restarts into charge-only mode: about 12-15 s after you put it down it changes from starting up to the breathing amber charge light. Press Steam to switch it on as usual; lift it off the Puck and it switches off. Touching anything (any button, stick, trigger or grip) right after switching on keeps it running as before.
- **The charge light no longer comes on in games** when the right stick is held to the right (from [v51.4.1](https://github.com/pablo89898991/sc26re-1khz/releases/tag/v51.4.1)).

## Under the hood

- The firmware writes only warnings and errors to its USB console (nobody reads the info lines), which frees 17.5 KB of flash for later fixes. Measured with the controller moved through its full range over the Puck: same report rate and lost reports as v51.4.1.
- The first 16 s after power-on are recorded for diagnosis (`sc26re/boottrace` in the report meter's feature paths).
