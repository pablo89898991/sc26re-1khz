Custom Steam Controller (2026) firmware with **1000 Hz** reports and the 1 kHz IMU path, based on Valve's latest stock firmware **6AE90BC5**.

**Download:** `SC26RE_1k.fw` is the firmware to use. Also attached:
- `SC26RE_1k_capsense_dbg.fw`: raw stick/grip capsense values in the quaternion fields, for tuning.
- `SC26RE_1k_puck_dbg.fw`: Puck link counters in the quaternion fields.

Flash with Ibex Web Utilities or `flash.py`. The report meter is on the [SC2 1 kHz tools](https://pablo89898991.github.io/sc26re-1khz/) page; refresh it (Ctrl+F5) after updating.

## Changes since v43

### Report meter works while Steam is running
- **The problem:** the report meter and Steam share one command channel to the controller, and the controller's replies carry no addressee. With Steam open, the meter could get Steam's reply, fail with "Failed to write / receive the feature report", or show empty Live settings.
- **Tagged replies:** replies to sc26re's own settings now start with a tag derived from the setting name. The meter recognises a reply that was meant for Steam and asks again.
- **Retries:** reads are retried up to 8 times. Over the Puck the meter also waits for the reply to arrive by radio before asking again. Writes are retried up to 6 times.
- **Result:** Read all, Apply, Save, gyro calibration and the other tools work with Steam open, over the cable and over the Puck.

### Controller picker for the Puck
The Puck shows up as 5 HID devices, one per controller slot. The meter now shows a **"Controller"** list with each one and how many controller reports per second it delivers (e.g. "#3 — controller (998 reports/s)"). It picks the right one automatically, and you can change it. All commands go only to the chosen one.

### Controller stays on while the meter is open
With **"Keep controller on"** ticked (the default), the controller no longer switches itself off for inactivity while the report meter page is open, even lying untouched over the Puck. When you close the page, or untick the box, the normal auto power-off returns. Steam and games do not affect this.
