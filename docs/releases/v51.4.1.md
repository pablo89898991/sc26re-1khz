Flash `SC26RE_1k.fw` with the report meter's flasher; your settings and profiles are kept. **[SC2 Overlay 1.4](https://github.com/pablo89898991/sc26re-1khz/releases/tag/overlay-v1.4.0)** (Windows and Linux) tells you when a new firmware is out.

## Fix

- **The charge light no longer comes on in games.** Holding the right stick to the right could turn the LED amber, as if the controller lay on the Puck. The firmware took a "Puck contact" reading from an input that turned out to be the right stick's X axis: measured on a controller, it reads the same on and off the Puck, and about 2.8 V with the stick held right. That reading is gone; the Puck is recognised by its link and by the charging power, as before.

## Known issue

- A controller that is **off** and placed on the Puck switches fully on instead of only charging. Switch it off again with Steam + Y if you only want it to charge. This will be fixed in a later release.
