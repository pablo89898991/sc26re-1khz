## New: accelerometer bindings in the remapper

In the [remapper](https://pablo89898991.github.io/sc26re-1khz/remap.html) there is a new **Accelerometer** entry under the controller picture, next to Gyro. Each profile has its own settings, and all of it works with or without Steam:

- **Tilt moves a stick:** tilting the controller pushes the left or right stick (to Steam or the Xbox pad), on top of the stick itself, for leaning or steering. **Sensitivity** in % (100 % = full deflection at 45° of tilt), a **dead zone**, invert X / Y, and swap (forward tilt on X).
- **Tilt presses:** tilting forward, back, left or right presses the **Tilt forward / back / left / right** bindings, in 4 or 8 directions. **Sensitivity** in % (100 % = a direction at 20° of tilt).
- **Gestures:** a quick jolt of the controller taps up to 2 actions each: jab forward, pull back, jab left, jab right, jolt up, jolt down and **shake** (a quick up-and-down shake). **Sensitivity** in % (100 % = a jolt of 1.5 g). A jab and its stop count once. A shake fires once, however long it goes on; its first jolt can also give its direction before the shake is recognised.
- **Off while the gyro mouse / joystick is on** (one tick box), so the gyro and the accelerometer don't fight.

The centre is how you hold the controller when the profile becomes active (switch profiles, or turn the remapper off and on, to centre it again). It doesn't drift, because it comes from gravity. Putting the controller down on a desk is a real jolt and can fire "jolt up" or "jolt down".

## Fixes

- **Gyro mouse too slow after Save on the gyro page** (for example after setting a response curve) until you calibrated again. During a sensor restart the firmware read the gyro range back at a moment when the sensor briefly ran at another range, then kept converting for that range. The conversion now comes from the range that is set, and a wrong range in the sensor is put back.
- **The accelerometer could report twice its real value after switching on** (1 g read as 2 g and clipped). Steam and games saw the wrong values, and motion gestures were cut off. Same cause, same fix.
- **No pad reports at 250 or 500 Hz without Steam** while the gyro mouse (or an accelerometer binding) kept the motion sensor on. Games reading the controller directly saw a frozen pad. Reports now come at the set rate.
- **Accelerometer range ±4 g** (was ±2 g), so harder jolts are not cut off.
- **False gestures after a gyro setting change:** the motion sensor gives wrong readings for a moment twice after a range change or a Save. Gestures ignore those readings now, and tilt keeps its last position through them.

## Tested on a controller

- 229 remapper cases (every action, button mode, trigger, stick and trackpad mode, shift, layer, macro, gyro in the profile, settings and Steam Input imports), 100 random profiles and 11 diagnostic cases. Every result is the same as the simulator's, apart from a few that differ by 1 ms at a step edge.
- Firmware test page: every setting, every report rate (also without Steam), every gyro / accelerometer range and rate: 172 of 172.
- Gyro regression (Save / Apply, ranges, rates, sensor modes, profiles, gyro mouse): 25 of 25. Accelerometer: 16 of 16 test cases, no false gestures on a still controller, and real tilts, jabs and shakes by hand.

## Under the hood

- `sc26re/acc/<profile>`: the accelerometer settings (32 bytes) per profile.
- `sc26re/imumode`: also the accelerometer conversion and how often its range was put back.
- `sc26re/usbdbg`: counters of the settings requests the controller received.
- The hardware test pages (`hwtest.html`, `fwtest.html`) and the remapper test tools now send one request at a time and wait for each, so they no longer lose the connection for minutes.
