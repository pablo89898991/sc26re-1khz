Custom Steam Controller (2026) firmware with **1000 Hz** reports and the 1 kHz IMU path, based on Valve's latest stock firmware **6AE90BC5**.

**Download:** `SC26RE_1k.fw` is the firmware to use. Also attached:
- `SC26RE_1k_capsense_dbg.fw`: raw stick/grip capsense values in the quaternion fields, for tuning.
- `SC26RE_1k_puck_dbg.fw`: Puck link counters in the quaternion fields.

Flash with Ibex Web Utilities or `flash.py`. The report meter is on the [SC2 1 kHz tools](https://pablo89898991.github.io/sc26re-1khz/) page.

## Changes since v42

### Gyro calibration measures instead of guessing (fixes drift after calibrating)
- **The problem:** "Calibrate gyro" (in the report meter and in Steam) used to copy the IMU's sensor-fusion drift estimate, like stock. That estimate is only right after the controller has been still for a while. Pressed too early, it saved a wrong drift, and the gyro drifted afterwards, even after a restart.
- **What it does now:** the firmware **measures** the gyro for ~1.5 s and saves the average as the drift.
- **Movement check:** if the controller moves, the old calibration is kept. Movement is judged by the standard deviation (threshold ~0.86 °/s), so single glitch samples no longer make a still controller fail.
- **Report meter:** shows the result ("calibrated and saved" / "moved — old calibration kept") and the measured noise per axis.

### Full gyro calibration with a button chord
**How to run it:** hold **LB + RB + X + Y for 3 seconds**. The LED turns **purple** and a chime plays. Then **put the controller on a table and let go**. The same routine also runs from **"Full gyro calibration"** in the report meter.

**What it does, about 15 s in total:**
1. Measures the gyro at rest for 5 s and sets the **pitch / yaw / roll dead bands** from it (the measured range ± 0.05 °/s).
2. Calibrates the gyro drift.
3. Measures at rest again for 5 s, now with the new drift, and sets the dead bands again (narrower).
4. Calibrates the gyro drift again.
5. Saves everything.

**Progress, so it never looks frozen:**
- **Waiting for you to put it down, or touched:** the LED blinks slowly in purple and a low beep sounds every 2 s.
- **Measuring:** the LED blinks fast. There are no beeps while measuring, because the grip actuators would shake the gyro.
- **After each step:** a short beep that gets higher with every step.
- **Done:** the "paired" chime plays and the LED returns to its previous colour.

**If something goes wrong:**
- Touching or moving the controller just repeats the current step once it is still again.
- If it cannot finish within 60 s, the power-off sound plays and the old calibration stays.

**No Steam needed:** the controller switches its IMU on by itself for the calibration.

### Automatic gyro calibration when the controller is put down
**When it runs:** when the controller has lain untouched for **3 s** (adjustable 1–30 s, 0 = off) while the gyro is in use. "Untouched" means no hands on the grips, sticks or trackpads and no buttons pressed. The controller then quietly re-measures the drift.

**Safeguards:**
- **Held still in your hands:** it does not run, because the grip sensors detect your hands.
- **Lying on something that moves slightly:** on a lap or a sofa the measurement is rejected and tried again later.
- **Once per put-down:** after a successful calibration it waits until you pick the controller up again.

**What it changes:**
- Tiny changes (under ~0.03 °/s) are ignored.
- Flash is written at most every 5 minutes, or at once for a big change (over ~0.3 °/s). In between, the new drift is used until power-off.
- It is silent, with no LED change, and leaves the dead bands alone.

### Gyro dead bands per axis: pitch, yaw, roll
New rows in **Live settings** let you set a range per axis, "from … to" in °/s, that reads as 0, for example pitch −0.3 to 0.3 and roll −0.2 to 0.7. Uneven ranges are allowed, and 0 and 0 turns a band off. The names and the order match Steam's Gyro Calibration page: pitch, yaw, roll.

### Gyro at rest measurement in the report meter
**"Measure 5 s at rest"** shows the lowest, highest and average gyro value per axis. The dead zones are switched off while it measures and then restored. **"Use as dead bands"** copies the result, plus a margin, into the dead band rows.

### Report meter works without Steam
- **"Gyro on (standalone)"** (on by default) switches the controller's IMU on from the page, the same way Steam does. Gyro graphs, calibration and measurements no longer need Steam's gyro page open. The IMU is switched off again when the page is closed.
- **Puck:** commands now go only to the Puck interface the controller's reports come from. The Puck shows up as 5 HID devices, and writing to the wrong one failed with "Failed to write the feature report".
- **Reliability:** all commands to the controller are sent one at a time, with automatic retries.
- **Close Steam while changing Live settings:** Steam and the page share the same command channel, and their replies can get mixed up.
