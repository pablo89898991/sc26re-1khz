## New

- **Choose where trigger and gyro haptics play.** In the remapper (Settings → Haptics and sounds, and the gyro mouse section): on the grips, on the trackpads (Valve's choice) or on both. It applies to Steam's haptics and the firmware's own, whatever you are touching. "Both" plays the grip first and the trackpad 80 ms later: played at the same moment, the grip could not be felt.
- **Firmware trigger haptics.** A tick, click or strong pulse when a trigger reaches a point you set (default 85 %), without Steam.
- **Firmware rotational haptics for the gyro.** A tick every few degrees of turn (1 to 45, default 5), without Steam. When they are on, Steam's rotational ticks are dropped, so they can also be used to turn those off.
- **Remapper at power-on.** "As it was last" (Steam + Menu is remembered) or "always off", so every power-on starts in Steam mode.
- **Battery status** in the report meter, the remapper and SC2 Overlay.
- **Charge light on the Puck.** A running controller placed on the Puck now breathes amber while it charges, steady amber for 10 s when full, then its normal colour. Lifted, it shows its colour again within about 3 s, and that is your remapper profile's colour if one is on. Switching the remapper on or off while the controller lies on the Puck takes effect once it is lifted. The amber has its own brightness, so it still breathes smoothly with the LED brightness set low.

## Fixes

- **Grips that turn on and stay on.** The grip sensors drift up after some play; the firmware now follows that hands-off drift (Settings → Touch sensors → Grip drift tracking, default 60 %, 0 = off), so a grip no longer stays pressed, and the old 8 s release that could cut a real hold short is gone.
- **No lag on the Puck.** Putting the controller on the Puck while playing no longer makes it stutter.

## Known issue

- A controller that is **off** and placed on the Puck switches fully on instead of only charging. Switch it off again with Steam + Y if you only want it to charge. This will be fixed in a later release.
