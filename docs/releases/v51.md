Custom Steam Controller (2026) firmware with **1000 Hz** reports and the 1 kHz IMU path, now with a **full remapper that runs on the controller itself**: keyboard, mouse, Steam pad and Xbox 360 pad outputs, without Steam Input.

**Download:** `SC26RE_1k.fw` is the firmware to use. Also attached:
- `SC26RE_1k_capsense_dbg.fw`: raw stick/grip capsense values in the quaternion fields, for tuning.
- `SC26RE_1k_puck_dbg.fw`: Puck link counters in the quaternion fields.

**How to flash:** use the flasher in the report meter so that your pairings and settings survive. Close Steam first, connect by cable and keep "keep settings" ticked. The tools are on the [SC2 1 kHz tools](https://pablo89898991.github.io/sc26re-1khz/) page; refresh it (Ctrl+F5) after updating. v51 doubles the settings storage: a backup from v50 or older is restored into the new layout automatically.

**Windows overlay app:** [SC2 Overlay](https://github.com/pablo89898991/sc26re-1khz/releases/tag/overlay-v1.1.0) shows the on-screen menus and the 3D controller in a see-through window over your game (details below).

## Key combinations

**Steam + D-pad** ↑ → ↓ ← profile 1–4 · **Steam + Menu** remapper on/off · **Steam + View** gyro mouse on/off · **Steam + A** 360° calibration · **Steam + X** (3 s) gyro calibration · **Steam + Y** power off · **Steam** alone 7 s power off. While Steam is held these buttons never reach the game, and Steam never sees the Steam press.
At power-on (hold while pressing Steam): **RB + A** Puck mode · **RB + B** or **LB + B** Bluetooth + pairing · **Menu + View + LB + RB** (3 s) factory reset.

**Changed from v50:** the gyro mouse (LB + RB + View), its 360° calibration (LB + RB + View + A) and the gyro calibration (LB + RB + X + Y) moved to the Steam button, because LB and RB reached the game before the combination was complete. Steam held alone now powers off after 7 s (was 5 s), and never while another button is held with it.

## Changes since v50

### On-controller remapper (new)
Open the **Remapper** page on the tools site (desktop Chrome, Edge or Brave), connect, edit, **Save to controller**. Works over the cable, the Puck and Bluetooth.
- **4 profiles** (Steam + D-pad, or a "switch profile" action), each with its own name, LED colour and, optionally, its own gyro settings. **Action layers** (a profile laid over the current one while held) and **mode shifts** (another mode for a stick, pad, trigger, D-pad or the face buttons while a button is held).
- **Outputs:** any keyboard key or modifier (up to 4 at once), mouse buttons 1–5, wheel, mouse movement, Steam pad buttons / sticks / triggers, **Xbox 360 pad** (see below), profile switches, layers, **macros**.
- **Activators like Steam Input:** regular, long, double, start and release press, toggle, turbo, cycle, chords, delay start / end, interruptible, per-binding haptics and timing.
- **Sticks:** analog with dead zones, anti dead zone, response curve, invert / rotate; D-pad 4/8-way with **outer ring** (and **"ring key first"**: the ring key, e.g. Shift for sprint, goes down before the direction); mouse, scroll, flick stick, radial menu, mouse region, single button, Xbox stick.
- **Trackpads:** mouse with trackball and edge spin, joystick, mouse joystick, D-pad, gates, 3 × 3 grid with movable lines, scroll wheel, directional swipe, radial menu, mouse region, single button; touch, click or **pressure** activation, zone haptics.
- **Triggers:** simple threshold, hair trigger, hip fire, hip fire exclusive, analog range to Steam or Xbox.
- **Gyro:** as mouse or joystick, tilt to directions, mouse region, player / world space, activation by several buttons (or touch), ratchet, smoothing, per-profile range.
- **Steam Input import / export (.vdf):** your Steam layouts come in with their action sets, layers and mode shifts. Steam's D-pad layout (four / eight way) and "touch the stick = mode shift" are carried over.
- **Presets**, e.g. "Steam Controller (whole pad)" and "Xbox pad"; **unbound inputs send nothing** option per profile.
- While the remapper is on, the Steam button never reaches Steam (bind it to "Pad Steam" to give it back).

### Xbox 360 pad output (USB)
Optional **Pad output** (top right of the remapper page): *Steam Controller*, *Xbox 360 only* or *Steam + Xbox 360*, over the USB cable. Games that read XInput get a real Xbox 360 pad, with **rumble** on the controller's haptics. Steam lists it as an unknown "Valve Corporation SC26RE-1kHz" pad: no setup needed.

### On-screen menus and overlay
- The **Overlay** tab of the remapper shows radial menus and touch menus (3 × 3 grids) with what each slice does (or your own names), the chosen slice lit, and the profile's name when it changes. Place, size, colours and background (including transparent) are set in folding columns; **Arrange** lets you drag everything into place.
- **3D overlay** page: Valve's official model (or a detailed textured one) following the controller, buttons, sticks, pads, triggers, back buttons and grip sense, popped-out triggers / bumpers / back buttons, gyro speed and drift correction, frame-rate limit. Shows what your hands do even when the remapper changes the output.
- **SC2 Overlay for Windows:** a transparent, click-through window over the whole screen, always on top (games in a window or borderless full screen). Tray icon: Arrange (Ctrl+Shift+F10), show / hide (Ctrl+Shift+F9), remapper and overlay settings, screen, start with Windows.

### Calibration and settings
- **Stick calibration** (range and centre) and **trackpad pressure calibration** from the remapper and the report meter.
- **Trigger full-range calibration**, gyro drift / full calibration, gyro mouse 360°, all on the remapper's Calibration tab.
- Settings storage doubled to **32 KiB**. Report meter: **Back up settings only** (a .bin file you can restore later), besides the backup made when flashing.
- The remapper page has the report meter's **Live settings** (triggers, report rate, trackpads, touch thresholds, haptics, power and LED).

### Puck
- Keyboard reports and a **5-button mouse** reach the PC through the Puck (the Puck patcher adds full keyboard and mouse to every Puck version).
- Bluetooth pairing works with the stock chord (Steam + RB or LB + B).

### Gyro and timing
- IMU samples read straight from the bus (two Zephyr patches): the gyro reaches the report sooner, and the remapper pass takes about 1.2 ms instead of 1.9 ms.
- Report rates 250 / 500 Hz keep their schedule (were 223 / 411 reports per second).
- Fixed gyro jumps in Steam (the IMU timestamp could go backwards).

### Fixes
- View and Menu were swapped in the firmware's names: fixed, the report is the same as stock.
- A key toggled on could stay held at the PC after power-off over the Puck: the remapper now lets go of everything first.
- The controller's Steam combinations no longer open Steam's menu or Big Picture.
- Serial shell off in release builds.

### Testing
Every change was checked on a host build of the remapper (simulator, scenario tests, fuzzing, web page tests) and on the controller with the hardware test page: the controller plays scripted inputs and its output is compared with the simulator (224 cases, including real community Steam layouts).
