Custom Steam Controller (2026) firmware with **1000 Hz** reports and the 1 kHz IMU path, based on Valve's latest stock firmware **6AE90BC5**.

**Download:** `SC26RE_1k.fw` (`SC26RE_1k_puck_dbg.fw` is the same build with Puck link counters in the quaternion fields). Flash it with Ibex Web Utilities or `flash.py`. The report meter and the patcher are on the [SC2 1 kHz tools](https://pablo89898991.github.io/sc26re-1khz/) page. For 1 kHz over wireless, patch your own Puck firmware (`PROTEUS_FW`) in the [web patcher](https://pablo89898991.github.io/sc26re-1khz/patcher.html), or take a pre-patched stock pair from the `stock-1k-…` releases.

## Changes since v27

### Triggers
- **No calibration needed after flashing.** At power-on, the firmware works out each trigger's direction from its resting position. Before, the right trigger could read as pressed after a flash because of a wrong factory flag, so you had to press both triggers once after every boot.
  - Until the direction is decided, the trigger defaults to "rests high", which is correct on these controllers.
  - The ADC warm-up samples are skipped before the check.
- **Direction stays fixed.** Once decided, the trigger direction is kept even when Steam rewrites the calibration records.
- **Stock-compatible trigger mode.** Setting 62 `trigger_mode` is now implemented like stock (1 = millivolts, 2 = raw ADC, no digital click in either).

### Trackpads
- **Full pressure range.** Pad pressure now reaches **0–32767** like stock. It used to stop at about 8191 because the pressure curve was clamped at the calibrated maximum. The click threshold is unchanged.

### Haptics
- **Cleaner sound on the grips.** The grip actuators' audio stream and amplifier now keep running for 150 ms after the last effect, and the output buffer is 3 blocks deep. Back-to-back effects no longer restart the stream with a pop. This affects Steam's "Identify controller" (Ping), multi-step scripts, PCM / Fancy Haptics, and fast series of effects. Grip haptics may be about 8 ms later.
- **Sine phase is wrapped** like stock, which keeps long tones precise.

### LED
- **Charging indicator on the Puck.** When the running controller sits on the Puck, the LED slowly breathes amber. It stays steady amber for 10 s once fully charged, and your LED colour comes back when you lift the controller.

### Tools
- **Report meter:**
  - Trigger view shows raw mV, the active calibration and `trigger_mode`.
  - The Steam command log shows every setting in each settings packet, not just the first one.
- **CI:** versioned releases now stay "Latest". The rolling `latest` build is published as a pre-release.

## Known issue (Steam bug, also happens on stock firmware)
If you open Steam Input from the Steam Guide while touching a capacitive sensor, Steam may treat the right trigger as held and the left trigger as dead. The controller still reports both triggers correctly. To clear it, press both triggers once inside Steam Input, or open *Settings → Controller → Test Device Inputs*.
